⌘01 / STRUCTURE

What does technical SEO check?

Find access, indexing, and metadata signals that deserve review.

  • Title / description / canonical / HTTPS
  • Robots directives and heading hierarchy
  • Links, images, and semantic HTML
✳02 / CLARITY

What does AI readiness mean?

Check declared search access, extractable text, and identification present in the HTML.

  • Search crawler rules
  • Extractable text and main content
  • Declared authors, dates, and markup
≡03 / USEFULNESS

How is the content structured?

Inspect headings, lists, and information available in HTML. The audit does not judge search intent or answer quality.

  • Headings and first paragraph
  • Observed lists, tables, and numbers
  • Structural signals, without semantic diagnosis

What the tool does.
And how to use the results.

What is GEO?+

GEO, or Generative Engine Optimization, describes practices that make content more accessible and understandable in AI search experiences. This audit focuses on technical and editorial signals in HTML, based on SEO fundamentals. Read the practical guide.

Does this check whether my business appears in ChatGPT?+

No. The tool does not query ChatGPT, Gemini, or other assistants, or track Google rankings. It evaluates page readiness signals. A good score does not guarantee indexing, rankings, or citations.

Does it audit the entire site?+

Each audit considers the HTML returned by one URL and public supporting files such as robots.txt and a sitemap. It does not crawl all pages or run JavaScript. Content loaded only in the browser may not be detected.

How are scores calculated?+

Scores are heuristic: weighted checks create an area summary. Results help prioritize review but do not replace human audits, traffic data, or performance tests. Inspect the evidence, not just the number. Understand the criteria.

Do I need an account? Are reports saved?+

There is no account or server-side history. Clicking save or import stores one baseline in this browser for comparison. It persists across reloads and language changes and can be deleted. Imported files are validated locally, without upload. Operational logs may exist; see the privacy policy.

Why might an audit fail?+

The page must be public, accessible over HTTP or HTTPS, and return HTML. Bot protection, authentication, request limits, network errors, or slow responses may prevent access. Internal and private addresses are not accepted.

How do SEO and GEO differ in this tool?+

SEO reviews technical fundamentals. AI readiness adds search crawler access, extraction, and content identification signals. They overlap; neither score measures actual rankings or citations.

How long does an audit take?+

Timing depends on the site and supporting files. The browser waits up to 120 seconds. You can stop waiting; the server may finish a request already in progress.

What is included in the free audit?+

One public URL at a time, SEO and AI readiness scores, all checks, priorities, and exports. Daily usage limits apply. You can also save a browser baseline and compare a new audit.
