What the tool does.
And how to use the results.

What is GEO?+

GEO, or Generative Engine Optimization, describes practices that make content more accessible and understandable in AI search experiences. This audit focuses on technical and editorial signals in HTML, based on SEO fundamentals. Read the practical guide.

Does this check whether my business appears in ChatGPT?+

No. The tool does not query ChatGPT, Gemini, or other assistants, or track Google rankings. It evaluates page readiness signals. A good score does not guarantee indexing, rankings, or citations.

Does it audit the entire site?+

Each audit considers the HTML returned by one URL and public supporting files such as robots.txt and a sitemap. It does not crawl all pages or run JavaScript. Content loaded only in the browser may not be detected.

How are scores calculated?+

Scores are heuristic: weighted checks create an area summary. Results help prioritize review but do not replace human audits, traffic data, or performance tests. Inspect the evidence, not just the number. Understand the criteria.

Do I need an account? Are reports saved?+

No account is needed. The application does not keep a persistent report history. Download JSON or Markdown locally, or save a PDF through your browser. Infrastructure and servers may keep operational logs; see the privacy policy.

Why might an audit fail?+

The page must be public, accessible over HTTP or HTTPS, and return HTML. Bot protection, authentication, request limits, network errors, or slow responses may prevent access. Internal and private addresses are not accepted.
