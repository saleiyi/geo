Privacy, clearly explained

What happens to the URL you submit and the data used to produce your report.

Updated September 26, 2026 · MandAPI GEO team

What do we receive?

When you start an audit, your browser sends the submitted URL to the server. The server accesses that public page and supporting files to extract technical and content signals. The destination site receives these requests and may log them.

Submit public URLs only. Do not include passwords, access tokens, personal data, or confidential information in the address or its query parameters.

Are reports stored?

The application does not use a database to maintain an audit history. Information is processed during the request, and the report stays in the memory of the open page. Reloading or closing the page removes it from the interface.

JSON, Markdown, or PDF files you export are saved to your device according to browser settings. They may contain URLs and public excerpts from the audited page; consider their contents before sharing.

Cookies, analytics, and AI services

This interface includes no third-party analytics, advertising pixels, or first-party tracking cookies. Audits started through this form use the rules engine without an AI-generated summary.

The API may offer an optional AI summary, used only when requested and configured by the operator. In that case, report information may be sent to the configured provider. This website’s form does not enable that option.

Logs and infrastructure

The server, hosting provider, proxies, and protection services may keep operational records such as IP addresses, timestamps, routes, errors, and request information. Retention depends on deployment configuration. No report database does not mean no logs exist.

To apply usage limits, the application keeps an IP-derived identifier in memory using a hash and a temporary random salt. The counter resets at midnight UTC and is not persisted in a database. Attempts that begin an audit also count when reading fails. Effective limits depend on the service configuration.

A deployment-dependent policy

This page describes how the application works. The operator’s identity and contact channel, hosting provider, and retention periods must be supplied by the operator before public operation requiring those details. We do not invent an operator or retention period.

Review this page again if integrations, accounts, or storage are introduced. The description must stay aligned with the service’s actual configuration.
